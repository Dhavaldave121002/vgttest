Discovery & Requirement Analysis

Website Planning & Structure

03

Frontend Development

Translating approved designs into pixel-perfect, responsive code using semantic HTML5, modern CSS/React standards, touch-friendly interfaces, and clean component architecture.

07

Testing & Quality Assurance

Conducting rigorous cross-browser testing across Chrome, Safari, Firefox, and Edge, along with mobile responsiveness, form security checks, and broken link scans.

08

Deployment & Handover

Launching your live website on your verified hosting environment, setting up SSL certificates, submitting XML sitemaps to Google Search Console, and providing administration walkthroughs.
